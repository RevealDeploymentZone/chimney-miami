About AL Chimney Service Miami

Miami's trusted chimney and fireplace experts. Certified professionals, advanced equipment, and honest service since 2010.

Your Trusted Chimney Service Partner in South Florida

AL Chimney Service Miami has been providing professional chimney and fireplace services to homeowners and businesses throughout Miami-Dade County and South Florida for over a decade. Founded on principles of safety, quality workmanship, and transparent customer service, we have built our reputation one chimney at a time by delivering the thorough, professional service that Miami property owners deserve.

Our team of certified chimney professionals understands that your chimney is a critical safety system in your home. Whether you use your fireplace regularly during Miami's brief winter season or only occasionally, proper maintenance and timely repairs are essential to preventing chimney fires, carbon monoxide exposure, and costly structural damage. We take our responsibility seriously, and we approach every service call with the same attention to detail and commitment to safety that we would want for our own families.

Why Miami Homeowners Choose AL Chimney Service

When you choose AL Chimney Service Miami for your chimney and fireplace needs, you are choosing a company that prioritizes your safety and satisfaction above all else. Here is what sets us apart from other chimney service providers in South Florida:

Certified and Trained Professionals

Our chimney sweeps and technicians are certified by the Chimney Safety Institute of America (Chimney Safety Institute of America (CSIA)), the gold standard for chimney service professionals. Chimney Safety Institute of America (CSIA) certification requires completion of intensive training programs, passing comprehensive examinations, and maintaining ongoing education to stay current with the latest chimney technology, safety standards, and best practices. When a CSIA-certified sweep arrives at your door, you can be confident that they have the knowledge and skills to inspect, clean, and repair your chimney system safely and effectively.

In addition to CSIA certification, our team receives ongoing training in National Fire Protection Association (National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) 211) 211 211 standards (the National Fire Protection Association's standard for chimneys, fireplaces, vents, and solid fuel-burning appliances), Miami-Dade County building codes, and manufacturer-specific training for fireplace and stove systems.

Advanced Equipment and Technology

We invest in the best tools and equipment available to chimney service professionals. Our service vehicles are equipped with professional-grade rotary brush cleaning systems, high-powered HEPA-filtered vacuum equipment, high-definition video inspection cameras, moisture meters, thermal imaging cameras, and specialized tools for every type of chimney repair. This investment in technology allows us to provide more thorough cleaning, more accurate diagnosis, and higher-quality repairs than companies using outdated equipment.

Miami Climate Expertise

Miami's subtropical climate presents unique challenges for chimney systems that are not encountered in other parts of the country. Year-round high humidity, salt air from the Atlantic Ocean and Biscayne Bay, intense UV exposure, tropical storms, and hurricane-force winds all accelerate wear on masonry and metal components. Additionally, Miami's mild climate means that fireplaces are used sporadically rather than heavily, which creates its own maintenance challenges.

Our team has extensive experience working with Miami's diverse housing stock, from historic masonry chimneys in Coral Gables to modern prefabricated fireplaces in new Doral and Kendall developments. We understand the specific issues that affect South Florida chimneys, and we know how to address them effectively.

Comprehensive Service Offering

AL Chimney Service Miami is a full-service chimney company. Unlike companies that specialize in only one aspect of chimney service, we handle everything in-house, from routine annual sweeping to complex chimney rebuilding projects. This comprehensive approach means you have one trusted partner for all your chimney needs, and you are never passed off to subcontractors or referred elsewhere for specialized work.

Our services include:

  • Chimney sweeping and cleaning
  • Level 1, 2, and 3 chimney inspections
  • Chimney repair and masonry work
  • Chimney relining (stainless steel, aluminum, and cast-in-place)
  • Chimney cap and crown repair
  • Chimney leak diagnosis and repair
  • Chimney waterproofing
  • Fireplace cleaning, repair, and installation
  • Wood stove, gas fireplace, and pellet stove service
  • Dryer vent cleaning
  • Animal and nest removal
  • Commercial chimney services
