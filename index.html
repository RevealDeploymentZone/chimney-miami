Complete Chimney & Fireplace Services in Miami

AL Chimney Service Miami offers a full range of chimney and fireplace solutions for residential and commercial properties throughout Miami, Miami Beach, Coral Gables, and the greater South Florida region. Every service is performed by certified technicians using professional-grade equipment.

Chimney Sweeping [400x250]

Chimney Sweeping

Professional chimney sweeping removes creosote, soot, ash, and debris from your flue system. Creosote, a byproduct of burning wood, is highly flammable and its accumulation is the leading cause of chimney fires in Miami homes. Our certified sweeps use industry-standard rotary brushes, vacuum systems, and inspection cameras to clean your chimney safely and completely without creating a mess in your home. Annual chimney sweeping is recommended by the NFPA and required by many Florida homeowner insurance policies.

Chimney Inspection [400x250]

Chimney Inspection

A thorough chimney inspection is the foundation of safe fireplace operation. AL Chimney Service Miami performs Level 1, Level 2, and Level 3 inspections in accordance with NFPA 211 standards. Our Level 1 inspections cover accessible portions of the chimney structure. Level 2 inspections, required for home sales and after chimney events, include video scanning of the flue interior. Level 3 inspections address serious structural concerns. We provide detailed written reports and photographic documentation of all findings.

Chimney Crown Repair [400x250]

Chimney Crown Repair

The chimney crown is the concrete or mortar slab that covers the top of the chimney, sealing it except for the flue opening. Cracked or deteriorated crowns are a primary entry point for water into the chimney system. Water infiltration leads to spalling bricks, damaged liners, and costly structural repairs. AL Chimney Service Miami applies professional crown repair coatings and rebuilds severely damaged crowns to create a watertight seal that withstands Miami's heavy tropical rains and extreme UV exposure year-round.

Chimney Leak Repair [400x250]

Chimney Leak Repair

Water leaking from a chimney can cause extensive damage to ceilings, walls, insulation, and the chimney structure itself. Common sources of chimney leaks in Miami homes include damaged flashing, a cracked crown, missing or deteriorated cap, porous masonry, and failed mortar joints. AL Chimney Service conducts a systematic leak diagnosis and addresses the root cause, whether it requires flashing repair, crown sealing, masonry waterproofing, or a combination of treatments. We stop the leak and prevent recurrence through Miami's heavy rainy season.

Chimney Waterproofing [400x250]

Chimney Waterproofing

Miami's intense summer rainstorms, high humidity, and salt-laden ocean air make chimney waterproofing an essential preventive maintenance step. AL Chimney Service applies professional-grade vapor-permeable waterproofing sealants to brick and mortar chimney exteriors. Unlike paint, masonry waterproofers allow moisture that has already penetrated to escape while blocking new water from entering, preventing spalling and efflorescence. Waterproofing extends the lifespan of your chimney and is far more economical than masonry repair or rebuilding in South Florida's harsh climate.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Chimney Service in Miami

How often should I have my chimney swept in Miami?

The National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) recommends that chimneys, fireplaces, and vents be inspected at least once a year and cleaned as needed. In Miami, where high humidity and salt air from the ocean can accelerate moisture damage and corrosion, annual chimney sweeping is especially important to prevent creosote buildup and structural deterioration. If you use your fireplace frequently, you may need service more than once per year.

What does a chimney inspection include?

A professional chimney inspection by AL Chimney Service Miami includes a visual examination of the firebox, smoke chamber, damper, flue lining, chimney cap, crown, and exterior masonry. We check for creosote buildup, cracks, blockages, water damage, and any structural issues that could pose a fire or carbon monoxide hazard. Level 2 inspections include video scanning of the interior flue and are required before the sale of a home.

Do you provide 24/7 chimney services in Miami?

Yes. AL Chimney Service Miami is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. Whether you need emergency chimney repair, a same-day inspection, or after-hours service, our team is ready to respond across all Miami neighborhoods and the greater South Florida area. Call us anytime at +1 786-509-6627.

How do I know if my chimney needs repair?

Signs that your chimney may need repair include: white staining (efflorescence) on the exterior brickwork, crumbling mortar joints, a damaged chimney crown, water leaks in the fireplace, a strong odor from the fireplace when not in use, visible cracks in the flue tiles, smoke backing up into the room, or excessive soot around the firebox opening. If you notice any of these signs, contact AL Chimney Service Miami immediately at +1 786-509-6627.

What is chimney relining and why is it important?

Chimney relining involves installing a new liner inside your chimney flue. Liners protect the surrounding masonry from heat and combustion gases, improve draft efficiency, and prevent carbon monoxide from seeping into your home. Relining is required when the existing liner is cracked, deteriorated, or missing, or when upgrading to a new heating appliance with a different flue size requirement. A properly lined chimney is essential for safe fireplace or stove operation.

Can you remove animals or birds nesting in my chimney?

Yes. AL Chimney Service Miami provides safe and humane animal and nest removal from chimneys. Common intruders in Miami chimneys include birds, raccoons, squirrels, and iguanas. After removal, we clean and sanitize the flue and install a properly fitted chimney cap to prevent future animal entry. Do not attempt to light a fire if you suspect an animal or nest is present in your chimney.

Do you offer dryer vent cleaning in Miami?

Yes. Our dryer vent cleaning service removes lint buildup from your dryer duct system. Clogged dryer vents are a leading cause of house fires in the United States. We recommend professional dryer vent cleaning at least once per year, or more frequently for households with heavy laundry loads. Signs of a clogged dryer vent include clothes taking more than one cycle to dry, excessive heat from the dryer, or a burning smell during operation.

Does Miami's tropical climate affect my chimney?

Absolutely. Miami's high humidity, salt air, tropical storms, and hurricane-force winds place exceptional stress on chimney masonry and metal flue components. Moisture intrusion, mortar deterioration, and corrosion of metal parts are all accelerated in South Florida's subtropical climate. Regular professional maintenance — ideally once a year — is critical to keeping your chimney safe, structurally sound, and code-compliant. AL Chimney Service Miami specializes in addressing these South Florida-specific challenges.
